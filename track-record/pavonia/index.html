Pavonia

An early lesson in execution risk and duration.

Location
10506 Pavonia Drive, Houston, Texas 77095
Participation
An early investment made independently by Alexander Vitenas
Financing
No debt
Expected investment period
Approximately six months
Actual investment period
Approximately two years
Outcome
Fully remodeled and sold

The original plan

Alexander acquired the property independently, with no investment partner and no debt. The plan was straightforward: renovate the house and sell it.

A longer path

Litigation with the former owner materially extended the investment. After the dispute was resolved, Alexander completed a full remodel and sold the property.

The investment lesson

Pavonia made duration risk tangible. A transaction can look attractive at acquisition while an unresolved problem commits capital and attention far longer than the original business plan anticipates.

The experience influenced how Alexander subsequently evaluated the path between acquisition and exit—not only what could go right, but what could delay the investment when it did not.
