Wimberley

Ownership research, an auction deadline, and a path to closing.

Location
Wimberley, Texas
Property
8.88 acres
Participation
Sourced and led by Alexander Vitenas with an investment partner
Outcome
Sold intact, without subdivision
Approximate acquisition price
$220,000
Approximate sale price
$400,000

The prices are approximate transaction figures, not net profit or investment-return calculations.

An approaching auction

Alexander discovered the opportunity approximately two weeks before its scheduled tax auction.

Using technology and research methods he had developed, he investigated the ownership situation and located the deeded owner’s grandson. Establishing whom to contact required substantial research into the property’s ownership history.

Establishing a contractual path

Alexander negotiated a purchase contract and delivered the transaction to a title company.

With the property under contract and title work underway, he persuaded the taxing authority to postpone the scheduled auction. The postponement created time to complete title work, document the private loan, and close.

Introducing private leverage

This was the first transaction in which Alexander and his investment partner incorporated private leverage into their investment model, adding financing to a process that already included sourcing, ownership research, negotiation, and title coordination.

Outcome

The investment was acquired for approximately $220,000 and ultimately sold for approximately $400,000.

The 8.88 acres were sold intact. No subdivision was carried out.

What the transaction demonstrates

The work involved finding the opportunity, reconstructing the ownership situation, locating the person needed to transact, and creating enough time to complete the purchase.

It also marked a change in the investment model through the introduction of private financing.
