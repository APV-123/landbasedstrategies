Real estate investment, informed by research and execution.

Land-Based Strategies represents the real estate investment work led by Alexander Vitenas.

Based in Houston and focused principally on Texas, the investment practice pursues opportunities where price, property characteristics, information, or a transaction problem creates a reason to look more closely.

The investment comes first.

An attractive opportunity can take different forms: land with subdivision potential, a complicated ownership situation, a poorly marketed property, or a commercial investment with a compelling thesis.

Participation can mean leading an acquisition, investing with a partner, or making a selective investment alongside another sponsor or operator.

The question is whether the property, price, structure, and work required justify committing capital.

Land. Capital. Code.

Land

Understand the property: its ownership, access, physical characteristics, possible uses, and marketability.

Capital

Consider the purchase and the path back out together. Price, financing, carrying costs, duration, and the eventual buyer or operating use all matter.

Code

Build or adapt tools around the problem. Software can collect information, organize a research universe, test a question, and preserve work that would otherwise need to be repeated.

Systems developed through investing.

Building technology around investment problems has been part of Alexander’s work for years.

Bespoke scrapers, SQL databases, analytical queries, and research workflows preceded the later application called Deal Studio. They were already part of finding and evaluating investments.

Deal Studio emerged from the effort to make more of that work persistent: opportunity research, prior analysis, contacts, decisions, and follow-up.
