Land-Based Strategies

Land-Based Strategies is the public-facing identity for real estate investment work led by Alexander Vitenas.

Based in Houston and focused principally on Texas, it brings together selected investment experience, operating methods, and the use of technology around real estate problems.

An investment practice with more than one form of participation

The work includes investments made independently, investments made with partners, and selective participation alongside other sponsors or operators.

The opportunity determines the appropriate involvement. Direct acquisition and execution are part of the practice, alongside the decision to commit capital to a compelling investment led by someone else.
